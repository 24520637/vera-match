# VERA Match — planned acceptance demo

1. Register a synthetic member account → open its activation email in Mailpit → verify → log in → reload to confirm session restoration → log out and log back in.
2. Complete the member profile, three questionnaire answers, reciprocal preferences, and five separate consent controls.
3. Upload a **synthetic or explicitly authorized test video** to private MinIO storage. Confirm another account cannot access it.
4. A moderator assigns an eligible reviewer. The expert sees only the assigned task and authorized video.
5. The expert records timestamped observations across five categories, saves drafts, and submits a review with limitations.
6. Independent QA approves or returns the review. The owner sees only an approved report and can comment, dispute, confirm, share, or revoke each eligible claim.
7. Reciprocal matching displays permitted reasons without revealing private raw notes. Both members accept, producing a mutual connection.
8. Blocking or withdrawing consent makes affected recommendations or playback access unavailable under the documented policy.
9. Show real test logs, negative-case evidence, known limitations, and P1 functionality that remains unimplemented.

> This is a **future demo script**. Do not present any item above as functioning until the corresponding ticket passes its UI/API/DB/test acceptance checks.
